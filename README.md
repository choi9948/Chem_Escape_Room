# Trinitro Trouble
3D Chemistry Escape Room
<br>**Unity Link:** https://play.unity.com/en/games/18ee9ded-f099-422a-8f58-19072cb0953d/trinitro-trouble

## Overview
The digital escape room explores key concepts from all major chemistry units covered in SCH4U course provided through TDSB: 
- Organic Chemistry
- Structures and Properties of Matter
- Thermochemistry & Chemical Kinetics
- Equilibrium
Each room in the escape sequence is built with increasing difficulty, combining storytelling, interaction, and curriculum content.

## Story Line
### Room 00: Introductory Puzzle
**Objective:** Retrieve the door knob to exit
- The player starts in a white room, nothing but a bed, a desk, a toilet, and security cameras in all four corners.

### Room 01: Organic Chemistry
**Objective:** Blindly synthesize N,N-dimethylpropanamide without proper safety measures.
- The chemical that is being made, presents a real danger as the room is poorly ventilated
  - Short-term effects: headache, fatigue, nausea, respiratory irritation, lightheadedness
  - Long-term effects: slow reaction, impaired judgment and coordination, disorientation

### Room 02: Structures and Properties of Matter
**Objective:** Retrieve phosphoserine from locked storage to restore health.
- Restore cognitive clarity
- Support memory, learning, concentration
- Prevent cognitive decline

### Room 03: Thermochemistry and Chemical Kinetics
**Objective:** Experiment and collect data to determine the optimal chemical to create a highly explosive substance (Trinitrotoluene - TNT).

### Room 04: Equilibrium (Planned)
**Objective:** Disrupt the equilibrium in the self-sustaining environment in the greenhouse, triggering the emergency exit and escape
